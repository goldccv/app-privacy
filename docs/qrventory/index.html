QRventory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

QRventory does not collect any personal data. All information you record — sites, cards, photos, videos, voice notes and transfer packs — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

Nothing. QRventory has no account system, no server, no analytics SDK and no tracking of any kind.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only to scan QR labels you create.
  • Microphone / Speech — used only to record your own voice notes; recognition runs on-device when supported.
  • Photo Library (add only) — used only when you choose to save your own photos.
  • Face ID — used only to lock the app if you enable it; biometric data never leaves the device.

Data sharing

Your data is never sold, shared or transmitted. Export packs you create are shared only by your own action (AirDrop, mail, etc.).
